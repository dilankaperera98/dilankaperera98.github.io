Research

MBA Dissertation · University of Wolverhampton · 2026

Impact of AI-Generated Content and Deepfakes on Consumer Trust in Sri Lanka: The Mediating Role of Perceived Risk

Investigated the relationship between exposure to AI-generated content and consumer trust in Sri Lankan social commerce, using survey data (N = 115) and mediation analysis. Findings indicated that perceived risk mediated the relationship between exposure and trust in the study sample.
